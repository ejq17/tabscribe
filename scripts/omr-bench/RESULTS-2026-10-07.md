## Known gaps

- Meter inference confuses 6/8 and 3/4 (lieder-zumsteeg-romanze: pitch F1 90% but time accuracy 0%).
- Measure over-count on lieder-zumsteeg-kapelle (26 vs 21) and mutopia-bach-menuet-g (33 vs 32).
- Chart m119: a tiny hollow notehead is missed.
- Degraded scans sit at about 25% pitch F1 on average; several pieces over-count measures (hensel 29/12, zumsteeg-romanze 23/12, schroeter 10/4) and lose key/time signatures.
- Two voices on one staff: rhythm is not separated per voice (affects full F1 on polyphonic pieces such as the Bach menuet).
- The "guitar" octave setting (read treble clefs an octave lower) defaults on, so it is applied to music that is not guitar unless the user turns it off.
- Chord OCR on degraded scans reads about 41% exactly (52% roots; londonderry-air 0%), and a bar the recognizer drops also drops its chord.
- Meter override now needs at least 4 bars and 75% agreement, so lieder-satie-chanson (2/4) keeps its misread signature (time accuracy 100% -> 73%, pitch F1 54% -> 51%) instead of being overridden wrongly elsewhere.
- OCR assets (worker, SIMD cores, eng.traineddata) are served locally from `public/ocr`, about 10 MB, and are not precached by the service worker (cached on first use).
- Chord labels in the browser can differ from Node on borderline words (A Child Is Born m38 `D7#9#5`: Node 0.50, Chrome-rendered canvas `D7#9b5` 0.37) because Chrome rasterizes the PDF slightly differently; the OCR runtime itself matches Node.
